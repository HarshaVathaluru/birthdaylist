MY JOURNEY

Engineering was always the first choice.

INFOSYS OFFER ₹25K / month
↓
JOINED VIDEOCON ₹8K / month
“I chose engineering because I wanted to remain an engineer.”

In 2006, after graduating as a Mechanical Engineer, I had an offer from Infosys paying around ₹25,000 per month. I chose instead to join Videocon for around ₹8,000 per month because I wanted to remain in core engineering.

At the time, I was often told that I had made the wrong choice. I even remember being told that some drivers earned more than I did. But engineering was never a fallback for me. It was what I wanted to pursue.

Years later, during the rapid growth of IT hiring around 2021, I began seeing civil and mechanical engineers with two or three years of experience leave core engineering for careers that appeared better paid, more visible and more aspirational.

That raised a deeper question: What happens to engineering and manufacturing when talented engineers stop seeing core engineering as a career worth pursuing?

THE AMBITION

To help core engineering and manufacturing regain the respect, opportunity, and aspiration they truly deserve.
